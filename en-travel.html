Opération Mermoz

Seeing the world at human scale, then keeping trace of it.

Why this name

Jean Mermoz crossed continents to connect worlds that knew nothing of each other. On a far more modest scale, Opération Mermoz is my own way of doing the same: setting off, looking closely, learning a language or a landscape, then coming back with something to tell.

01 · Notebooks

Keeping trace

Every expedition leaves behind notes taken on the spot — a word of vocabulary, a certain light, a sentence overheard along the way. These notebooks are the raw material for everything that follows.

02 · Cultures & Languages

Learning to see differently

A new language, a different way of working the land or inhabiting a landscape: these are windows that force you to reconsider what you thought was settled back home.

03 · Expeditions

Walking to the limit

A ridge, a summit, a multi-day trek: physical effort always brings you back to the essential, and it's often up there that the clearest ideas take shape.

04 · Flight

Gaining height, quite literally

Seeing mountains and valleys from a small aircraft permanently changes how you look at a territory you thought you already knew well.
