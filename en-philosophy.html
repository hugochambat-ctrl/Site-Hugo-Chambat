A quiet quest

I like to think of philosophy as a permanent construction site. It begins by dismantling the obvious, questioning certainties, and pushing every idea to its limits — sometimes to the point of absurdity. Then comes the time to rebuild: to choose, to understand, and to build, stone by stone, a more conscious way of inhabiting the world, of acting with others, and of giving meaning to our existence.

"If my work allows someone, if only for an instant, to feel this beauty — then it will have reached its goal."
