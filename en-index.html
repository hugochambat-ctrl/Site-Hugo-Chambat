Manifesto

I believe we grow a little every time we taste something greater than ourselves — a work of art, a summit, an idea… Exploring these encounters, then turning them into music, into stories, into shared moments: that, I think, is the whole of my work.

Five territories, one same gesture

Music is only one door among others. Each of these territories feeds into the rest.
